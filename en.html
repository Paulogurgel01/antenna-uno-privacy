Antenna Uno TV — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026 · Samsung Smart TV app, version 2.0.0

1. Who is responsible

RADIO ANTENNA 1 S.R.L., Via Bocca di Leone, 36, Roma, Italy, VAT number IT03621271000, is the controller responsible for the processing described in this notice. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact info@antenna1.fm or write to the address above. Telephone: +39 06 69921735. Website: antenna1.fm.

2. Scope and audience

This notice covers Antenna Uno on Samsung Smart TVs. The app offers radio and related media without an account, login or payment. It is intended for a general audience and does not impose an 18-year minimum age. A content age rating does not remove privacy protections for children. Parents or legal guardians can contact us about a child's data. Where applicable law requires parental authorization for particular processing, that requirement still applies.

The app does not ask for a name, birth date, email address or payment details to listen. Information you voluntarily send to our support or privacy email is processed to respond to your request.

3. Information processed

  • App-generated identifier: a random identifier is created and stored on the TV. It is included in requests to the Antenna 1 Hub and may be associated with interactions and advertising impressions. It is not Samsung's TIFA advertising identifier or a hardware serial number. Although it does not contain your name, it can distinguish one app installation from another.
  • Network and request information: IP address and technical information accompanying requests to the Hub, streaming servers and other content providers. Ad-impression records can include an approximate country, region and city inferred from the IP address. This is not GPS location.
  • Interactions: votes on music, likes on clips and search terms sent when you use those features. A list of liked clip identifiers is also stored locally on the TV.
  • Advertising events: ad and pre-roll views and ad clicks, together with relevant campaign, creative and app identifiers. Advertising impression records can also contain the app-generated identifier and the approximate location described above.
  • Correspondence: the contact details and information you choose to include when contacting us.

This Samsung version does not request access to the microphone, camera or precise GPS location. Firebase Analytics is not configured in this version.

4. Purposes and grounds for processing

We process the technical information necessary to deliver requested audio, video, images and app functions, respond to support requests and maintain the service. Votes, likes and searches are processed when you choose to use those functions.

Advertising-related information is used to deliver ads, count impressions and clicks, limit repeated displays and produce campaign statistics. An IP-derived location can be used in those records.

Under the controller's existing privacy policy, processing necessary to perform contractual service obligations is based on those obligations. Information voluntarily sent by email is used to respond to the sender and handle the request. Optional services for which consent is required are subject to the applicable consent requirements. Legal obligations may also require processing. Merely opening this app does not replace consent where the law requires it.

5. Local storage and your choices

The app stores its random identifier and liked clip identifiers in local storage. You can clear these using the TV's available app-data controls; doing so can reset preferences and cause a new identifier to be generated. Local entries have no automatic expiry in the inspected version. Clearing local storage does not by itself delete records already held by the Hub.

Voting, liking clips, searching and contacting support are optional. Network requests needed to load or play content occur when you use the app. Contact us to request access, deletion or information about server-side records.

6. Recipients and external services

Authorized personnel and service providers may process information needed to operate the app, its Hub backend, hosting, streaming and support. The app connects to the Antenna 1 Hub and to media and image hosts specified by that service. Radio playback uses a Newradio streaming endpoint. The interface also requests fonts from Google's font services. These connections transmit the IP address and normal technical request information to the relevant provider.

Independent websites opened from links and services operated by Samsung have their own privacy notices. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary for establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives technical connection information, including an IP address, when you visit it. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

7. International processing

Because the service uses electronic communications and service providers, information may be processed abroad, within or outside the European Union. In accordance with the controller's existing privacy policy, transfers outside the EU must comply with applicable law, using agreements providing adequate protection and/or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses where necessary. For information about the safeguards relevant to your data, contact info@antenna1.fm.

8. Retention and deletion

Personal information is retained for the time necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations and the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. This criterion also covers requests from supervisory authorities and the exceptions provided by applicable data protection law.

Local identifiers and likes remain until local app data is cleared or otherwise removed by the device. Clearing local data does not itself delete server-side records. You may request deletion of personal information through info@antenna1.fm, subject to the applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

9. Your rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data and object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. These rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in the applicable law.

Send requests to info@antenna1.fm. Explain the request and the app involved; we may need proportionate information to locate the relevant records and verify authority, including for a guardian's request. You may complain to your competent supervisory authority, including Italy's Garante per la protezione dei dati personali: garanteprivacy.it.

10. Security and changes

The controller's existing policy provides for technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No networked service can guarantee absolute security. This notice may be updated when the service or its data processing changes. Please check the date above and consult the latest version.
